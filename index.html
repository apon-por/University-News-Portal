University Hosts Annual Technology & Innovation Festival

Students Showcase Innovative Projects

The university successfully organized its annual Technology and Innovation Festival this week. Students from different departments participated in the event and presented innovative projects based on technology, engineering and communication.

The event provided students with an opportunity to demonstrate their technical knowledge, creativity and problem-solving skills. Faculty members and industry professionals also attended the program and appreciated the students' work.

The university administration stated that similar activities will continue throughout the academic year to encourage innovation and practical learning among students.
